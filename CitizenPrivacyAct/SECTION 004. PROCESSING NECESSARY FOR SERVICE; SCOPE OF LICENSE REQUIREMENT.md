SECTION 4. PROCESSING NECESSARY FOR SERVICE; SCOPE OF LICENSE REQUIREMENT.

(a) This part does not require a license for personal data processed solely as reasonably necessary to provide a good or service the individual has affirmatively requested, consistent with the purpose-limitation standard already established under ORS 646A.578. A controller's characterization of processing as "necessary" is not controlling; targeted advertising, profiling, resale, and the training of a machine-learning or artificial- intelligence model are never processing necessary to provide the requested good or service, regardless of how a controller's terms describe them.

(b) All other processing requires a license under this part.
