SECTION 10. SUCCESSOR LIABILITY.

(a) Except as provided in Section 12, a successor entity is bound by every obligation this Act imposed on the controller from which it acquired personal data — including the license terms, tier classifications, and revocation rights established under Sections 4 through 8 — to the same extent as the original controller, effective automatically upon acquisition and without need for a new grant of license from the individual.

(b) A successor entity shall notify each affected individual of the change in control within 45 days, using the same means by which the original controller was required to communicate under this Act.

(c) This section applies regardless of how the acquisition is structured, and regardless of whether the successor entity characterizes itself as a new or unrelated entity. A de facto continuation of the controller's business with respect to the personal data is sufficient to trigger this section.
