SECTION 6. SCOPE OF CONSENT BY DATA TIER.

(a) For personal data outside the sensitive tier defined in [Module 2], a controller may rely on an opt-out framework: processing under Section 4(b) may proceed unless and until the individual revokes, consistent with the opt-out mechanisms already established under ORS 646A.582, including recognized universal opt-out signals.

(b) For sensitive-tier data — genetic, biometric, health, precise geolocation, and the data of an individual known to be under sixteen — no license under Section 4(b) is valid unless obtained through a freestanding, opt-in request that is not bundled with acceptance of general terms of service, and that specifically identifies the data category, the purpose, and any compensation offered.
