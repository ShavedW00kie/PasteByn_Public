SECTION 9. DEFINITIONS.

"Downstream holder" means any person or entity that receives personal data from a controller, by license, sale, merger, acquisition, or operation of law, including through a proceeding under Title 11 of the United States Code.

"Successor entity" means any downstream holder that acquires substantially all of the assets, data, or business of a controller, whether by merger, consolidation, stock acquisition, or asset purchase, and whether the transaction occurs in the ordinary course of business or through a proceeding under Title 11 of the United States Code.
