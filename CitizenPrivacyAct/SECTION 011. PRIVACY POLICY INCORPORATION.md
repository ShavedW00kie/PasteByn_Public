SECTION 11. PRIVACY POLICY INCORPORATION; RELATION TO OREGON'S UNLAWFUL TRADE PRACTICES ACT.

(a) Every controller subject to this Act shall incorporate the rights and obligations established under Sections 4 through 10 into its publicly disclosed privacy policy.

(b) Any offer, sale, license, or transfer of personal data inconsistent with a controller's disclosed privacy policy, or inconsistent with this Act, constitutes an unlawful trade practice under ORS 646.608, independent of and in addition to any remedy otherwise available under this Act.

(c) For purposes of any proceeding under Title 11 of the United States Code, a transfer of personal data inconsistent with this section constitutes a violation of applicable nonbankruptcy law.
